Extras

Add these after the movie name, for example curl "{{HOST}}/starwars?t=5m&bar=0". Use quotes when there is an &.

Playback options
AddWhat it does
?t=12:30Start 12 minutes 30 seconds in. t=90 and t=1h2m work too.
?cols=…&rows=…Centre the picture and send the sharpest version that fits your window.
?style=asciiClassic text characters instead of HD colour pixels.
?color=0Black and white, like the 1990s.
?bar=0Hide the progress bar.
?loopPlay forever. Good for an office screen.

See the full list from your terminal with curl {{HOST}}/ls.

Have a private screening key?

Private titles only show up with a key. The key is kept in this browser, and is only sent to {{HOST}} when you play something.

In a terminal, send it as a header: curl -sN -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR-KEY" {{HOST}}/ls
