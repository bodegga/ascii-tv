Privacy Policy

ascii-tv streams ASCII-art animations to your terminal or browser. It has no accounts, no cookies, no ads, no analytics and no third-party scripts. We keep short-lived server logs that contain a shortened (truncated) version of your IP address, never the full address, and we delete them within 14 days. We do not sell or share personal information. The details are below.

1. Who we are

ascii-tv (the "Service") is operated by Bodegga ("Bodegga", "we", "us"), a small security company. Our website is bodegga.net. The Service lives at https://tv.bodegga.net and also answers on plain http:// so that terminal clients such as curl can use it.

This policy covers the hosted Service only, not copies of the open-source software that other people run.

2. What we collect

We designed the Service to work with as little data as possible. This is everything we handle.

Stream logs

When a stream starts and when it ends, the server writes a log line to the system journal on our server. It contains:

  • the name of the movie requested;
  • a truncated client network: the first three parts of an IPv4 address (a /24 network) or the first 48 bits of an IPv6 address (a /48 network), never the full address;
  • the terminal size your client requested;
  • how long the stream ran; and
  • the time of the event.

Your full IP address, in memory only

Any server on the internet has to see your IP address in order to reply to you. While your stream is open, the ascii-tv application holds your full IP address in memory so it can limit the number of simultaneous streams per address. This is an abuse-prevention measure. The application discards the address when the stream ends and does not write it to disk.

Web server access logs

A reverse proxy (nginx) sits in front of the application. Its access logs for this site, if any are kept, contain a truncated IP address, the request path (without the query string), the user-agent string your client sent, a timestamp and the response status.

Email you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it to answer you and to handle your request.

We do not obtain information about visitors from any other source.

3. What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-up, profiles or payments.
  • No cookies.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, advertising or device fingerprinting.
  • No third-party scripts, fonts, embeds or content delivery networks. The web player is served entirely from our own server.
  • No selling of personal information, and no sharing of it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • No use of your data to train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.
  • No profiling and no automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects. The only automated control is a limit on simultaneous streams per IP address.

4. Settings stored in your browser

The web player may save convenience settings in your browser's localStorage: font size, the last movie you watched and, if you enter one, an access key. This data stays on your device. It is not sent to us, with one exception: an access key is sent to our server when you use it to play a private title.

You can delete these settings at any time by clearing site data for tv.bodegga.net in your browser. Terminal clients store nothing on our behalf.

5. Access keys

Some titles are private and play only with an access key that we give out individually. Our server holds the list of valid keys so it can check them, and we may keep a note of who we gave a key to so that we can manage and revoke it.

A key can be sent in two ways: in the URL as ?key=…, or in an Authorization: Bearer header. The application never logs query strings, so a key sent in the URL is not written to the application's logs. Even so, URLs tend to be saved in places we do not control, such as browser history, shell history and bookmarks. We recommend that you:

  • send the key in the Authorization header where you can; and
  • use https:// whenever you use a key. Over plain http://, the key travels unencrypted and can be read by networks between you and us.

Stream logs record the title watched. If a private title has been shared with only a few people, a log entry about it may be indirectly attributable to a key holder even though it contains only a truncated IP address.

6. How we use information, and why

We use the information above only to:

  • deliver the stream you asked for;
  • keep the Service secure and available, including detecting, investigating and blocking abuse;
  • understand in aggregate how the Service is used, for example which movies are watched and for how long, so we can keep it running well;
  • check access keys for private titles;
  • reply to messages you send us; and
  • comply with the law and protect our legal rights.

If we have to block abusive traffic, we may keep a block-list entry for a network range, or for a specific address seen during an active attack, for as long as the block is needed.

If the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies to you, our legal basis for this processing is our legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) in operating a free service securely and preventing abuse. We limit the impact on you by truncating IP addresses and deleting logs quickly. Where we must process data to meet a legal obligation, that obligation is the legal basis.

7. How long we keep it

  • Full IP address held by the application: only while your stream is open. Never written to disk by the application.
  • Stream logs (system journal): up to 14 days.
  • Web server access logs: rotated and deleted within 14 days.
  • Email correspondence: as long as needed to handle your request and keep reasonable business and legal records.
  • Browser settings: on your device until you clear them.

We may keep specific records for longer if the law requires it, or if we need them to investigate a security incident or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

8. Infrastructure providers

We rely on a small number of companies to run the Service. They process connection data as infrastructure providers, under their own terms and privacy policies:

  • DigitalOcean hosts our virtual server in the United States (New York region). Network traffic to the Service, including your full IP address, necessarily passes through its network.
  • ZoneEdit runs our DNS. Your device or DNS resolver contacts it to look up our address.
  • Let's Encrypt issues our TLS certificates. Your browser or client may contact its systems to check a certificate's status.
  • Fastmail handles email sent to us.

We do not give these providers our logs for their own use, and we use no advertising or analytics providers.

9. When we disclose information

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We have not done either in the past 12 months.

We disclose information only:

  • to the infrastructure providers listed above, as needed to run the Service;
  • when we are required to by valid legal process, such as a subpoena, court order or warrant;
  • when we believe in good faith that it is necessary to protect the security of the Service, or the rights, property or safety of Bodegga, our users or the public; or
  • to a successor, if the Service or Bodegga is reorganized, merged or sold. This policy would continue to apply to information we had already collected.

We respond to valid legal process. In practice we hold very little: truncated IP addresses in logs that are deleted within 14 days. We usually cannot identify an individual viewer from our records.

10. Security

We protect the Service by collecting very little, offering HTTPS, and sending a strict Content-Security-Policy with the web player. Anything sent over plain http://, including the movie you request, is visible to networks along the way.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of the Service or of data in transit.

11. Children and content

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because we have no accounts and ask for no personal details, we do not know the age of any viewer. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example by email, contact us and we will delete it.

The movies are unrated. Some include mild cartoon violence. Viewer discretion applies.

12. Your privacy rights

An honest limit: we usually cannot tell which records are yours

Our logs contain truncated IP addresses and no names, accounts or cookies. A truncated address is shared by many devices, so in most cases we cannot link a log line to you, and we will not collect more data just to make that possible. If you give us extra details, such as your network range and the approximate time of your visit, we will look, but we may still be unable to confirm that a record is yours. Since logs are deleted within 14 days, there is often nothing left to find. We will tell you plainly what we can and cannot do.

Email is different. If you have written to us, we can find, correct or delete that correspondence on request.

California

The California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the CPRA, gives California residents the right to know what personal information a business collects, to obtain a copy, to correct it, to delete it, to opt out of its sale or sharing, to limit the use of sensitive personal information, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. Whether or not that law applies to a company of our size, we describe our practices in its terms:

  • Categories collected: identifiers (a truncated IP address, an access key if you use one, and your email address if you write to us) and internet or other electronic network activity information (movie requested, watch duration, terminal size, user-agent, request path, timestamp).
  • Source: you and your device.
  • Purposes: those in section 6.
  • Sold or shared: none. This includes the information of anyone under 16.
  • Sensitive personal information: we do not collect it to infer characteristics about you.
  • Retention: as set out in section 7.

We recognize the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal as a valid opt-out of sale and sharing. Because we do not sell or share anyone's personal information, with or without the signal, it changes nothing in practice.

Other US states

Other state privacy laws give similar rights to access, correct, delete and obtain a copy of personal data, and to opt out of sale, targeted advertising and certain profiling. We do none of those three things. If we decline a request, you may appeal by replying to our decision, and you may contact your state attorney general.

European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland

Bodegga is the controller of the personal data described here. Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, you have the right to request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability, and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where we cannot identify you from the data we hold, these rights apply only if you provide additional information that makes identification possible (Article 11). You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.
